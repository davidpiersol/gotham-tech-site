Privacy Policy

Gotham Tech Solutions · New Mexico

Last updated September 21, 2026

Who we are

Gotham Tech Solutions is the public-facing brand of Gotham Consultants, LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with this website and inquiries about our services. Information processed for a client project may also be covered by the project agreement.

Information we receive

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number, service of interest and project description. Our inquiry form requests a name, email address, service and project description. Please do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information in an initial inquiry.

Website hosting and security services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, pages requested and request times.

How we use information

We use inquiry information to respond to you, discuss potential work and prepare proposals. We use technical information to operate and protect the website, identify automated submissions, prevent duplicate messages and investigate delivery problems. We may also use information as necessary to meet legal obligations or protect legal rights.

Service providers

Netlify hosts the website. Microsoft 365 processes inquiry email for delivery to our business inbox. Cloudflare Turnstile checks technical browser information to help distinguish people from automated submissions. Upstash stores keyed fingerprints, submission status and counters for duplicate prevention and rate protection; our application does not store message text or readable email addresses in that database.

These providers process information to support their services and may process it in locations outside your state or country. Their own privacy notices describe their handling of service and technical data: Netlify, Microsoft, Cloudflare and Upstash.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising pixels or marketing analytics on this website. Fonts and selected images are served with the site. Hosting and bot-protection services may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. Browser settings can affect how your device retains site data and autofill information.

How long we keep information

We review non-client inquiry email monthly and delete inquiries that have not become client projects after 12 months. If an inquiry becomes a client project, we explain applicable record-retention arrangements as part of the engagement. Provider backups or legal recordkeeping requirements may delay permanent removal.

Duplicate-protection records expire after seven days. Rate-protection counters expire after one hour or one day. Hosting and security providers maintain their own technical logs under their service policies.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about your information or request access, correction or deletion. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We handle requests subject to applicable law, including any legal requirement to retain records. Rights and response requirements can vary with your location and circumstances; nothing in this policy limits rights provided by law.

Security

We use safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted connections and controls intended to prevent abusive or duplicate submissions. No online service or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. We will provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
