Who we are

Gotham Tech Solutions is the public-facing brand of Gotham Consultants, LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company. This policy describes the current website preview. Separate project agreements may address information handled while providing client services.

Information in the inquiry form

The form asks for your name, email address, service of interest and project description. In this preview, those entries are processed in your browser to validate the form and display a summary. They are not sent to us or deliberately saved in cookies or browser storage. Use fictional details while testing. Your browser may independently retain autofill data or restore page state.

Email and telephone contact

If you email or call us, you choose what information to share, such as your contact details and project needs. We use that information to respond and discuss your inquiry. Selecting an email or telephone link opens your device’s configured application; the communication is handled by the services involved. Please do not include passwords, payment information or other sensitive details in an initial inquiry.

Website requests and cookies

The current preview does not include analytics, advertising pixels, social embeds or third-party booking tools. Fonts and images are served with the site. The preview does not deliberately set tracking cookies. Requests to load pages may appear in server logs and browser diagnostics, including technical information such as an IP address, requested page and time of access.

Service providers and disclosures

Website hosting and communications can involve service providers that process technical information or messages. The production provider list, processing locations and any additional disclosures must be confirmed before this draft is published as a live-site policy.

Retention and your requests

The demonstration form has no server-side submission record. Retention periods for correspondence, hosting logs and backups still need to be confirmed for the production service. You can contact us to ask about information you have provided or request correction or deletion. Applicable rights and any legal recordkeeping requirements depend on the circumstances; this draft does not limit rights provided by law.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy to reflect the actual services and information practices in use before enabling live form delivery. Future revisions will identify their effective date.
